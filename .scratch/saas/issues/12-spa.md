# The SPA

Status: ready-for-agent

Blocked by: 09

Vite + React in `apps/web`, served by the Worker through Static Assets — one
deploy, one origin, no CORS.

Screens: sign in; your Subscriptions with add and remove; Deliveries, showing
artist, title, outcome, and whether the match was exact. The Deliveries screen
is the product's answer to "why didn't it queue that song?", which today is
answerable only by reading scrollback.
